SOCIETYOS — QUICK START

1. Install Node.js 22 LTS or newer.
2. Extract the entire ZIP.
3. Open the societyos folder and double-click START.bat on Windows.
   On macOS/Linux/WSL: open a terminal in that folder and run:
   node scripts/start.mjs
4. Wait until both servers are ready, then open http://localhost:5173
5. Select an account card, enter the password printed in the terminal,
   and click Sign in securely.
   The password is also in data/LOCAL_DEMO_LOGIN.txt after first startup.

Keep the terminal open. First installation requires internet access.
The local data is persistent: do not delete the data folder.
There is no passwordless role login. All product pages require sign-in.

The dark sidebar groups the main screens. More in the top bar opens
related pages such as Buildings, Floors, Bookings and Payment review.
Sign out is at the bottom of the sidebar.
